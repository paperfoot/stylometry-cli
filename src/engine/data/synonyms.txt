# Synonym groups where the choice is a matter of voice, not meaning.
# One group per line, comma-separated, lowercase; multi-word members are
# matched as phrases. Inflected forms are their own groups (said / says).
# A writer who reaches for one member and never the others has a habit worth
# keeping; a draft that swaps in another member is drifting off-voice.
#
# Groups are deliberately narrow: members must be interchangeable in most
# contexts, and a word appears in at most one group. Polysemous words whose
# main sense is something else ("novel", "home", "like", "left", "so") are
# left out on purpose: a wrong suggestion is worse than none.
